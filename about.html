A LITTLE ABOUT SHALINI01 — 04

Confidence is not about becoming someone else.

It is learning to express what is already yours—with clarity, warmth, and conviction.

As a mentor at Mumbai Toastmasters and a keynote speaker, Shalini has helped more than 5,000 people strengthen their confidence. Her work brings public speaking and communication coaching together with personal image guidance, so people can feel prepared in the room and comfortable in their own skin.

Her approach is practical and encouraging: notice what is getting in the way, practise one skill at a time, and build habits that feel natural beyond the session.
